Protected information

Work with sensitive content deliberately

The Confidential area looks familiar, but its content is protected separately from ordinary workspace files and follows explicit access rules.

My Space

Your private confidential area. Create or upload sensitive material here when it is intended only for your account.

Shared

Items other people have shared with you appear here. What you can view, edit, or manage depends on the permission you received.

Secrets

Use this area for sensitive values such as access tokens. Secret values are treated differently from normal documents and should never be copied into ordinary files.

Permissions

Open an item's permissions before sharing it. Give only the access required, review who can use the item, and remove access when it is no longer needed.

Important: Moving through Confidential does not turn its items into normal files. dpuAgent protects the content and decides access according to the assigned roles.
