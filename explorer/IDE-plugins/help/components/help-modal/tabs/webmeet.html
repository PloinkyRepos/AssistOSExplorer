Live collaboration

Meet and work together inside the workspace

WebMeet combines live audio and video with chat, shared workspace resources, and a collaborative blackboard.

Create or join a room

Open WebMeet to see available rooms. Create a room for a new session or join an existing one. A guest invitation lets someone enter only the room you shared.

Prepare your media

Choose your microphone and camera before joining. During the meeting you can mute, stop video, share your screen, and adjust participant audio.

Use chat and resources

Send messages to the room and attach relevant workspace resources when available. Guests can use room resources without browsing your workspace.

Work on the blackboard

Add notes, polls, lists, drawings, and supported documents to a shared canvas. Everyone in the room sees updates as collaboration progresses.

Privacy tip: Share only the screen, room link, and resources you intend participants to see. WebMeet and its live-media services run as separate agents from Explorer.
