# AI Researcher

Looks for strong understanding of LLMs, agentic systems, evaluation methods, prompting, fine-tuning, RAG, model limitations, and AI safety. Should be able to study papers, test ideas experimentally, compare approaches, and transform research directions into practical prototypes.

## Key Traits

- Analytical thinking
- LLM knowledge
- Experimental discipline
- Scientific rigor
- Critical judgment
- Technical curiosity
- Strong documentation

## Engagement Path

Research collaboration opportunities, AI methodology testing, LLM evaluation pipelines, agentic system prototyping.
